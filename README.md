# 🧭 North Star Compass

> *Don't let today's fires burn down tomorrow's goals.*

## What is this?

North Star Compass is a lightweight web app that helps you stay aligned with your long-term strategy when short-term pressures pull you off course.

We're wired to firefight. A demanding request lands in your inbox and suddenly your long-term plan takes a back seat. This is **hyperbolic discounting** — our tendency to overvalue immediate rewards over future ones. North Star Compass acts as a strategic gut-check before you say yes.

## How it works

You provide two inputs:

| Input | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **⭐ Your North Star** | Your overarching long-term goal or core strategy | *"Transition from marketing to software engineering within 2 years"* |
| **⚡ The Immediate Action** | The specific problem or decision in front of you right now | *"Should I take on this high-paying freelance marketing gig this weekend?"* |

An LLM then analyses the tension between the two and gives you a clear, honest answer: does this action move you **toward** your North Star, pull you **away** from it, or is it genuinely **neutral**?

## Tech stack

- **[Astro](https://astro.build)** — fast, minimal frontend framework
- **Azure OpenAI / Gemini** — LLM reasoning layer
- **Stateless** — no login, no database, no friction

## Philosophy

Simple. Honest. Fast. The app should get out of your way and give you a clear signal in seconds.
